# Code of conduct

The project aims to provide a collaborative, safe, and respectful space for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Critique ideas and technical changes, not people.
- Consider feedback in good faith and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect privacy: do not publish personal information, private EVTX records,
  or credentials.

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information
without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these rules, decline
contributions, or temporarily restrict participation. To report misconduct,
contact a project maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not use public issues
to report sensitive information.

Enforcement decisions will be proportionate to the impact, and maintainers will
explain actions when practical.
